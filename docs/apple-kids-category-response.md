Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Kidzo. Please find our responses below.

1. Does the app include third-party analytics?
No. Kidzo does not include third-party analytics or transmit usage events to analytics services. Learning summaries are calculated on the device.

2. Does the app include third-party advertising?
No. Kidzo does not display advertisements or include advertising SDKs or ad networks. An ad-network policy link is not applicable.

3. Will data be shared with any third parties?
No. Kidzo does not transmit profile information, learning progress, drawings, or device identifiers to Annotex or third-party servers. There is no online account system or developer-operated backend. Profile records, progress, and preferences remain on the device. Drawings are saved to the device photo library only when the user chooses to save them. Any device or photo-library backups enabled by the device owner are managed separately through the device’s backup settings.

4. Is the app collecting user or device data for other purposes?
Kidzo does not collect user or device data on our servers. The app supports local profiles containing a name or nickname, age, and avatar selection. Activity results, scores, story progress, achievements, and preferences are stored on the device solely to provide profiles, personalize activities, resume progress, and display learning summaries. They are not used for advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Parents can access the privacy policy from Settings. Privacy and support inquiries voluntarily sent by an adult are handled through annotex.work@gmail.com, separately from in-app activity.
